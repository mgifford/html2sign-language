OPEN RESEARCH / EXPERIMENT P3

Browser AI English simplification

English only. This experiment may rewrite English into simpler English. It does not produce ASL glosses, signs, motion, or avatar output. The deterministic P1/P2 pages remain available without AI.

Research boundary

No ASL correctness, translation, accessibility-conformance, or avatar claim is made. The model is optional, local, consent-based, and evaluated on English preservation only. A malformed or uncertain response is a recorded failure, not a successful baseline.

One small browser-compatible model via Transformers.js. No hosted commercial API and no telemetry.
